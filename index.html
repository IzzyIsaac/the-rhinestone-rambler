Is the whole home ours for the stay?+

Yes. You’ll have the home to yourselves, except for locked cabinets, closets, and the tiki fridge where we keep personal belongings. This is our cherished home, so we ask you to treat it with the same care we do.

What are check-in and checkout times?+

Check in after 3 pm and check out before 11 am, Nashville local time. Self check-in is available with a smart lock; arrival details are shared through Airbnb.

The home is on one level, but every outside entrance has two steps to get inside.

Is the home suitable for children or pets?+

Well-behaved children are welcome, though we wouldn’t describe our home as particularly suitable for children because of the delicate decor, electronics, and tasteful adult artwork. Please keep these details in mind when deciding whether it’s the right fit for your family. Pets aren’t allowed.

What house rules should we know?+

No parties, events, smoking, or commercial photography. Only registered guests are allowed, with a maximum of six. Please respect our neighbors and quiet hours from 11 pm to 8 am.

Exterior security cameras monitor the entryways and remain on during stays. There are no indoor cameras. Please read the complete rules and camera disclosures on Airbnb before booking.

Can we book an extended stay?+

Monthly stays are welcome for guests in good standing. We open dates around our own travels, so if your preferred dates aren’t available, message us through Airbnb. We’re happy to see what might work.
